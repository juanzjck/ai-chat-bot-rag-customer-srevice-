# Rescheduling Payments and Financial Hardship

## Moving a Pay in 4 due date
For Pay in 4, you can move the due date of one installment per order by up to 7 days, free of charge. Do this in the Lumio app under the purchase by tapping "Change due date". The request must be made before the installment's due date.

## Moving a Monthly Financing due date
For Monthly Financing you can change your monthly payment day once every 12 months. The new day must be within 15 days of the current one.

## Financial hardship plans
If you cannot keep up with payments because of job loss, illness or another hardship, Lumio offers hardship plans that can lower or pause payments for up to 3 months with no late fees. Hardship plans are reviewed individually by a human specialist and cannot be approved by the virtual assistant.
