# Monthly Financing

## How Monthly Financing works
Monthly Financing spreads a larger purchase over 6, 12 or 24 monthly payments. It is available for orders from $250 to $10,000. Unlike Pay in 4 and Pay in 30, Monthly Financing can charge interest.

## Interest rates (APR)
The APR for Monthly Financing ranges from 0% to 29.99% and depends on the merchant offer and your credit profile. Your exact APR, monthly payment and total cost are always shown before you confirm the plan. The rate is fixed for the life of the plan.

## Credit checks for Monthly Financing
Checking whether you prequalify uses a soft credit check and does not affect your credit score. If you accept a Monthly Financing plan, Lumio performs a hard credit check, which may affect your credit score. Monthly Financing payment history is reported to credit bureaus.

## Paying off Monthly Financing early
You can pay off a Monthly Financing plan early at any time. There is no prepayment penalty, and you only pay the interest accrued up to the payoff date.

## Payment methods for Monthly Financing
Monthly Financing payments can be made by bank account (ACH) or debit card. Credit cards cannot be used to pay Monthly Financing plans.
