# Refunds and Returns

## How to return an item
Returns are handled by the merchant, not by Lumio. Follow the merchant's return policy and send the item back to them. Then open the purchase in the Lumio app and tap "Report a return" so that we can pause your payments.

## Pausing payments during a return
When you report a return, upcoming payments for that purchase are paused for 21 days to give the merchant time to process it. If the merchant has not confirmed the return after 21 days, the payment schedule resumes and you should contact the merchant or open a dispute.

## Refund timing
Once the merchant confirms the return, Lumio processes the refund within 5 to 7 business days. Money you already paid is sent back to your original payment method. Your bank may take a few additional days to show the credit.

## Partial refunds
If only part of an order is refunded, the refund amount is first deducted from your last remaining installments. If the refund is larger than your remaining balance, the difference is returned to your original payment method.

## Refunds of fees and interest
If an order is fully refunded, any interest charged on that order is refunded too. Late fees are not automatically refunded when an order is returned.
