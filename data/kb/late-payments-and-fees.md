# Late Payments and Fees

## What happens if a payment fails
If an automatic payment fails, Lumio retries the same payment method after 2 days. We notify you by email and push notification each time a payment fails. You can also pay manually in the app at any time.

## Grace period
Every installment has a 7-day grace period after its due date. If the payment is completed within the grace period, no late fee is charged.

## Late fee amounts
If an installment is still unpaid after the 7-day grace period, a late fee of $7 is added for that installment. Total late fees on a single order are capped at 25% of the order value. Pay in 30 purchases are charged a single $7 late fee if unpaid 7 days after the due date. Lumio never charges compounding interest on late fees.

## Account restrictions after a missed payment
While any payment is overdue, your account is paused and you cannot make new purchases with Lumio. The pause is lifted automatically as soon as the overdue amount is paid.

## Credit reporting and collections
Late Pay in 4 and Pay in 30 payments are not reported to credit bureaus. Monthly Financing payments that are more than 60 days late are reported to credit bureaus. Any balance unpaid for 90 days may be referred to a collections agency.

## Requesting a late fee waiver
Lumio can waive one late fee per 12-month period as a courtesy. Waivers cannot be granted by the virtual assistant; they must be requested from a human support agent, who reviews your payment history.
