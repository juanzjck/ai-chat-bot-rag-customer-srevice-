# Pay in 30

## How Pay in 30 works
Pay in 30 lets you receive your order first and pay the full amount up to 30 days after the item ships. There is no interest and no fee if you pay on time. Nothing is charged at checkout.

## Eligibility for Pay in 30
Pay in 30 is available for orders between $10 and $600 at participating merchants. You must be 18 or older and a US resident. A soft credit check is used, which does not affect your credit score.

## Extending a Pay in 30 due date
You can extend the due date of a Pay in 30 purchase one time, by 10 days, for a flat $3 extension fee. Extensions must be requested in the Lumio app before the original due date. An overdue purchase cannot be extended.
