# Contacting Support

## Support hours
The Lumio virtual assistant is available 24/7 in the app and on the website. Human support agents are available by chat and phone Monday to Friday 8am to 8pm Eastern Time, and Saturday 9am to 5pm Eastern Time. Human agents are not available on Sundays.

## Phone numbers
General support: 1-800-555-0142. Fraud line (24/7 for reporting unauthorized purchases): 1-800-555-0199.

## What the virtual assistant can and cannot do
The virtual assistant can explain how Lumio products, fees, refunds and disputes work. It cannot see your account, orders or balances, and it cannot change payments, waive fees, approve hardship plans or issue refunds. For anything that requires access to your account, it connects you with a human agent.

## Making a complaint
To make a formal complaint, email complaints@lumiopay.example or ask an agent to open a complaint case. Lumio acknowledges complaints within 2 business days and sends a final response within 15 business days.
