# Privacy and Data

## Requesting a copy of your data
You can request a copy of the personal data Lumio holds about you in Settings > Privacy > Download my data. We deliver the file within 30 days.

## Deleting your data
You can request deletion of your personal data after your account is closed. Lumio is legally required to keep transaction records for 5 years, so those records are retained and then deleted.

## Marketing preferences
You can turn off marketing emails and push notifications in Settings > Notifications. Payment reminders and other service messages cannot be turned off.
