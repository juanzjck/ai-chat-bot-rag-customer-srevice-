# Approvals and Spending Limits

## How approval decisions are made
Lumio makes an automated approval decision for every purchase. The decision considers your payment history with Lumio, the order amount, how long you have had an account, and any outstanding balance. Being approved once does not guarantee future approvals.

## Why a purchase was declined
Common reasons for a decline are an overdue payment, too many open plans, an order amount above your current spending power, or details that could not be verified. Support agents and the virtual assistant cannot override an automated decline.

## Spending power
Lumio does not give a fixed credit limit. Your estimated spending power is shown in the app and changes over time. Paying on time and paying off open plans usually increases it. New customers typically start with spending power of up to $600.

## Number of open plans
You can have up to 5 open Pay in 4 or Pay in 30 plans at the same time.
