# Pay in 4

## How Pay in 4 works
Pay in 4 splits a purchase into four equal, interest-free payments. The first payment (25% of the order total) is charged at checkout. The remaining three payments are charged automatically every 2 weeks, so the plan is fully paid in 6 weeks. Pay in 4 never charges interest.

## Eligibility for Pay in 4
To use Pay in 4 you must be at least 18 years old, live in the United States, have a valid mobile number, and have a debit or credit card. Orders must be between $35 and $1,500. Approval is decided for each purchase and using Pay in 4 only requires a soft credit check, which does not affect your credit score.

## Pay in 4 payment schedule
Payment 1 is due at checkout. Payment 2 is due 14 days later, payment 3 is due 28 days after checkout, and payment 4 is due 42 days after checkout. You can see every due date in the Lumio app under Purchases. We send a reminder by email and push notification 3 days before each payment.

## Paying off Pay in 4 early
You can pay any installment, or the full remaining balance, early at any time with no fee. Open the purchase in the Lumio app and tap "Pay early".
