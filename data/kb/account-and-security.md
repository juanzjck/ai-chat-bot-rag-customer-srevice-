# Account and Security

## Logging in
Lumio does not use passwords. You log in with your email address or mobile number and a one-time 6-digit code that we send by SMS or email. Codes expire after 10 minutes.

## Updating your phone number or email
You can update your email in Settings > Profile. To change your mobile number you must verify your identity with a code sent to your current email and the last 4 digits of a payment card on file.

## Unauthorized purchases
If you see a purchase you did not make, report it within 60 days in the app using "Report a problem" > "I didn't make this purchase", or call the fraud line. You are not liable for confirmed unauthorized purchases. Your account is locked while we investigate, which normally takes up to 10 business days.

## How Lumio will contact you
Lumio will never ask for your one-time code, full card number or bank password by phone, email or chat. If someone asks for these, do not share them and report it to support.

## Closing your account
You can close your account in Settings > Profile > Close account once all balances are paid in full. Accounts with an outstanding balance or an open dispute cannot be closed.
