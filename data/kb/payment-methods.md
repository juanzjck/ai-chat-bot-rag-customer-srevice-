# Payment Methods

## Accepted payment methods
Lumio accepts Visa and Mastercard debit cards, Visa and Mastercard credit cards, and US bank accounts via ACH. Prepaid cards and American Express are not accepted. Credit cards cannot be used for Monthly Financing.

## Changing your payment method
To change the card or bank account used for a purchase, open the purchase in the Lumio app, tap "Payment method" and select or add a new one. The change applies to all future payments of that purchase.

## Autopay
Autopay is turned on by default for Pay in 4. Scheduled payments are charged automatically on the due date. For Pay in 30 and Monthly Financing you can turn autopay on or off in Settings > Payments.

## Making a manual payment
You can pay manually at any time in the Lumio app or at lumiopay.example/pay. Manual payments made by card post immediately; bank account payments can take up to 3 business days to post.
