# Lumio One-time Card

## What the One-time Card is
The One-time Card is a virtual Visa card created in the Lumio app that lets you use Pay in 4 at online stores that do not offer Lumio at checkout.

## How to create a One-time Card
In the Lumio app tap "Shop anywhere", enter the store and the exact order total including tax and shipping, and confirm your Pay in 4 plan. A virtual card number is generated for you to enter at the store's checkout.

## One-time Card validity
A One-time Card is valid for 24 hours and can be used for a single purchase. If it is not used within 24 hours it expires, the plan is cancelled and nothing is charged.

## Where the One-time Card cannot be used
The One-time Card cannot be used for gambling, bill payments, gift card purchases, cash withdrawals, or recurring subscriptions.
