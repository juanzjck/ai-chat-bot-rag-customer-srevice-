# Disputes

## When to open a dispute
Open a dispute if you were charged the wrong amount, received a damaged or incorrect item, returned an item but got no refund, or never received your order. Always try to resolve the issue with the merchant first.

## Dispute deadline
A dispute must be opened within 120 days of the purchase date. Disputes opened after 120 days cannot be accepted.

## How to open a dispute
In the Lumio app, open the purchase, tap "Report a problem" and choose the reason. Upload any evidence such as photos, tracking numbers or emails with the merchant.

## What happens during a dispute
While a dispute is open, payments for the disputed amount are paused and no late fees are charged on it. The merchant has 14 days to respond. Lumio aims to resolve every dispute within 21 days and notifies you of the outcome by email.

## Dispute outcomes
If the dispute is resolved in your favor, the disputed amount is removed from your plan and any amount already paid is refunded. If it is resolved in the merchant's favor, your payment schedule resumes and the next payment is due 7 days after the decision.
