# Orders and Delivery

## Order not received
Shipping is handled by the merchant. If your order has not arrived, first contact the merchant for tracking information. If the order is still missing 10 days after the estimated delivery date, open a dispute in the Lumio app with the reason "I didn't receive my order".

## Cancelling an order
To cancel an order, contact the merchant directly. When the merchant cancels the order, your Lumio plan is cancelled automatically and any payment already made is refunded within 5 to 7 business days.

## Do payments start before delivery
For Pay in 4, the first payment is charged at checkout and the schedule continues even if the item has not arrived yet. For Pay in 30, the 30 days start when the merchant ships the order.
